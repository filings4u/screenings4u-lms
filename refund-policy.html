Refund Policy

This policy applies to Learning Center course purchases, group training products, course extensions, and training supplies.

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

1. Online Training Courses

Because online course access and digital training materials may be made available immediately, course purchases are generally non-refundable once the learner has accessed course content, downloaded training materials, begun an assessment, completed training activity, or received a certificate.

Before course access is used, you may contact support to request cancellation. Approved refunds may be subject to a $145 processing deduction.

2. Group Training

Unused group-seat purchases may be reviewed for cancellation before seats are assigned or training access is used. Once a seat has been assigned or used, the value associated with that seat is non-refundable. Custom live-training or organization-specific services may have separate cancellation terms communicated at scheduling.

3. Course Extensions

Course extensions are applied automatically to the associated enrollment after successful payment. Once the extension has been applied, the extension purchase is non-refundable except where required by law or when screenings4u confirms a processing error.

4. Training Supplies

Unshipped physical training-supply orders may be eligible for cancellation. Shipped supplies are generally non-refundable unless damaged, defective, or incorrect. Digital downloads included with a supply purchase are non-refundable once delivered.

5. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you believe you were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, contact us promptly. We will review the transaction and correct confirmed billing errors.

6. How to Request a Refund

Email support@screenings4u.com with the purchaser name, email address, order number, and reason for the request.
