If your screenings4u training access has expired—or you know you need more time—you can purchase a 30-day extension without signing in first.

Extend My Training Student Login

Use the same email address and last name associated with your original training purchase. If your enrollment was administratively suspended or cancelled, contact support instead of purchasing an extension.

HOW IT WORKS

Guest purchase, automatic account match.

  1. Open the secure extension checkout.
  2. Enter the email and last name used for your screenings4u Learning Center account.
  3. Complete the $100 payment through Stripe.
  4. Supabase links the paid order to your existing LMS enrollment.
  5. Your enrollment receives 30 additional days and becomes accessible again.
Already signed in?
You can also open My Courses and use the Extend button beside your course. Both paths use the same 30-day extension service.

Expired Training access does not disable your shared screenings4u identity or access to unrelated Testing, Workforce, or DOT services.
