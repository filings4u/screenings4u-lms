Learner orientation

Start here before beginning your training.

Your Learning Center keeps your courses, progress, order history, receipts, documents, mock-training submissions, appointments, and certificates connected to one learner account.

01

Navigate your Learning Center

My Learning contains courses you purchased or were assigned. Progress tracks completion. Certificates contains certificates issued to your account. Orders contains your Learning Center purchase history and receipts.

02

Support & scheduling

Use Training Support for course or account assistance. Use Schedule Appointment when your training requires a live session, appointment, or other assistance.

03

DOT mock collections

Applicable DOT Specimen Collector learners must complete the required five mock collection sessions. After completion, upload the supporting documentation in Documents for administrator review before certificate issuance.

04

Purchase & course access

Your course purchase, access period, extensions, and order history are tied to this account. Course extensions add time to the exact existing enrollment from which the extension was purchased.

05

Documents & certificates

Your Documents section contains acknowledgments, learner uploads, and files provided by screenings4u. Approved certificates are available from Certificates.

06

Refunds & legal information

Online training courses and digital products are final and non-refundable once purchased. Review the Refund Policy, Terms, Privacy Policy, and Training Disclaimer.
