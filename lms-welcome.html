Welcome to your training portal

Navigate your Learning Center

My Learning contains courses you purchased or were assigned. Progress tracks completion. Certificates contains issued certificates. Orders contains your Learning Center purchase history and receipts. Documents contains acknowledgments, uploads, and files sent to you by screenings4u.

Support & scheduling

Use Training Support for course or account help. Use Schedule Appointment when your training requires an appointment, live session, or assistance.

DOT mock collections

DOT Specimen Collector learners must complete the required five mock collection sessions. After completing them, upload the supporting mock-training documents in Documents. screenings4u reviews submitted documentation before certificate processing where applicable.

Your purchase & access

Your course purchase, access period, extensions, and order history are tied to your Learning Center account. Course extensions add time to the existing enrollment purchased by that learner; they do not create a different course enrollment.

Refund policy

Online training courses and digital products are final and non-refundable once purchased. Review the full Refund Policy before continuing.

Legal information

Training is educational and compliance-focused and does not replace legal, medical, tax, or other professional advice. Review our Terms, Privacy Policy, and Disclaimer.

Learning Center acknowledgment

Confirm your contact information and acknowledge the policies below. Your acceptance date and time are recorded with your account and a downloadable acknowledgment remains in Documents.
