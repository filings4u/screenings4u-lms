3. Employee Portal

This needs to remain intentionally simple.

You made an important distinction:

Screenings4u is not the employee's administrator. Their employer controls their access.

Exactly.

So the Employee Portal should not become a full customer portal.

Navigation
My Training

The employee sees:

Assigned courses
Courses in progress
Completed courses

Clicking a course sends them into the training platform.

Available Courses

This should only show courses their employer has made available to them.

Not necessarily the entire public course catalog.

For example:

Assigned Courses
───────────────
✓ DOT Collector Training
  In Progress

Available From Employer
───────────────────────
DOT Collector + Hair

Whether employees can independently purchase additional courses is a business decision for later, but based on your current structure, I would not put purchasing authority here by default.

Contact Employer

Simple area containing:

Employer name
Primary contact
Email
Phone number

Potentially:

Need help with your training?

Contact your employer administrator.

This reinforces that access is controlled by the employer.
