COURSE CURRICULUM

A structured curriculum covering the fundamentals of DOT specimen collection.

The training follows the major areas involved in DOT urine specimen collection procedures, collector responsibilities, documentation, and unusual collection situations.

01

Introduction & Background

DOT regulations, regulated agencies, safety-sensitive positions, testing reasons, panels, methods, and laboratories.

02

The Collector

Who may become a collector, training requirements, responsibilities, employer obligations, and collector restrictions.

03

Collection Site & Supplies

Collection-site requirements, donor privacy, security, required supplies, and collection-kit contents.

04

Custody & Control Form

DOT forms, required information, completion steps, signatures, and proper distribution.

05

The Collection Process

Collection steps, donor instructions, specimen inspection, splitting, sealing, and shipment preparation.

06

Monitored & Direct Observation

When monitored or observed collections are required and how those collections must be performed and documented.

07

Shy Bladder

Required shy-bladder procedures, donor instructions, timing, documentation, and next steps.

08

Problem Specimens

Temperature-out-of-range, adulterated, substituted, and other problematic specimen situations.

09

Unusual Collections & Mishaps

Refusals, missing signatures, catheter situations, fatal and correctable flaws, seals, and related issues.

10

Non-Regulated Testing

Key differences between DOT and non-DOT collections, including performing both for the same donor.

11

Starter Guide

Access a DOT Specimen Collector Starter Guide and supporting reference materials.

12

Policy Guides

Reference materials include sample DOT and NON-DOT Drug & Alcohol Policy guides.
